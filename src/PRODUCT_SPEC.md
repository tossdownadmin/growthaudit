# Product Spec — tossdown Restaurant Growth Audit V1

## Promise

Show a restaurant owner where growth is working and where customers, orders, or repeat visits are leaking.

## Audience

North American restaurant owners and operators.

## Flow

Search restaurant → confirm website/socials → lead capture → run audit → report.

No competitor selection and no questionnaire.

## Growth Engine Score — 100

1. Website + Ordering — 25
2. Reputation + Local Presence — 25
3. Customer Retention — 20
4. Customer Engagement — 15
5. Measurement + Growth — 15

Unknown evidence is excluded rather than scored as zero. Coverage below 85% is provisional.

## Owner questions

### Website + Ordering
Can customers easily buy from the restaurant directly?

Distinguish:
- owned ordering
- branded/direct ordering platform
- mixed direct + marketplace
- marketplace-only handoff
- unclear
- no ordering detected

### Reputation + Local Presence
When diners compare nearby options, is the restaurant winning trust?

Use:
- Google rating/review count
- recent sentiment
- website/search readiness
- lightweight local competitive benchmark

### Customer Retention
Can the first transaction become a second visit?

Public signals:
- loyalty/rewards
- customer account
- email capture
- SMS/WhatsApp
- app
- direct/branded ordering

### Customer Engagement
Is the restaurant visibly staying in touch?

Use:
- SocialCrawl posting activity/engagement
- Google review response behavior

### Measurement + Growth
Is there a public foundation to know what is working before scaling traffic?

Use:
- GA4 / GTM
- Meta Pixel
- Google Ads tags
- TikTok Pixel
- basic search readiness

## Competitive context

Do not ask the owner to pick competitors.

Build a small local comparison set automatically using Google Places, target category/concept evidence, distance, public reputation, and website ordering posture.

Do NOT run paid review/social/PageSpeed providers on competitors.

The benchmark is context, not the core product.

## Paid media readiness

Paid media is fuel, not the engine.

Report one of:
- Fix the engine first
- Almost ready to scale
- Ready to scale traffic

## Report order

1. Growth Engine Score
2. Five growth pillars
3. Biggest growth leak + top 3 priorities
4. Website + ordering relationship
5. Local competitive context
6. Google reviews/customer sentiment
7. Social activity/engagement
8. Paid-media readiness
9. Complete growth-engine map
10. Supporting technical evidence

## Tone

Restaurant-owner language. Commercial, concise, evidence-grounded.

Technical checks such as metadata, schema, pixels and Lighthouse remain supporting evidence, not the headline.
