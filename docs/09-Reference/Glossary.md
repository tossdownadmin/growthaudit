# Glossary

## Benchmark

Local competitive context calculated from selected nearby alternatives: rating, review volume, concept fit, threat, distance, and ordering posture.

## Branded direct ordering

Ordering hosted by a recognized restaurant commerce provider but presented as the restaurant's own direct customer path rather than a marketplace listing.

## Coverage

Percentage of weighted evidence that was actually measurable. Coverage communicates confidence separately from performance.

## Direct ordering

An ordering path where the restaurant has stronger control over branding, customer journey, and potentially customer data than on a marketplace.

## Evidence confidence

Assessment of whether enough reliable public evidence supports an exact competitor, review, social, or pillar conclusion.

## Growth Engine Score

Deterministic 0–100 weighted result across website/ordering, reputation, retention, engagement, and measurement.

## Marketplace ordering

Handoff to a marketplace such as DoorDash, Uber Eats, or Grubhub. It can deliver transactions but usually provides less customer-relationship ownership.

## Presentation eligible

Benchmark flag indicating candidates have enough V3 evidence to be described as likely direct competitors rather than only nearby reference points.

## Provisional

Overall audit state when evidence coverage is below 85%.

## Public evidence

Signals observable from Google, websites, social profiles, reviews, markup, links, or tracking code. It excludes private business systems and reporting.

## Relationship leak

A point where attention or a transaction does not lead into an owned/direct customer path or a visible repeat-customer mechanism.

## Substitution score

How plausibly a diner could choose a candidate instead of the target for the same occasion, product, cuisine, format, price, and geography.

## Threat score

Final competitor priority combining substitution, competitive strength, evidence, geography, and classification gates.
